About Drake

Drake is a Canadian rapper, singer, songwriter, and actor. He first gained recognition for his role as Jimmy Brooks on the television series "Degrassi: The Next Generation." Drake has since become one of the best-selling music artists of all time, known for hits like "Hotline Bling," "God's Plan," and "In My Feelings." He has won multiple awards, including Grammy Awards, and is considered one of the most influential figures in contemporary music.
