About Drake

Drake, born Aubrey Drake Graham, became one of the biggest names in music through his unique blend of rap and melody. Starting from acting before transitioning into music, he rose to global fame with projects like Take Care, Nothing Was The Same, and Views.

His influence on modern hip-hop and pop culture continues to shape the music industry worldwide.
